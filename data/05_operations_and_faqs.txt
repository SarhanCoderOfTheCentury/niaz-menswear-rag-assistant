# Operations Procedures and Frequently Asked Questions

This document describes how staff handle everyday tasks. Where it mentions rules about returns, delivery, payment, or warranty, the current policy document (Version 3.0) applies.

## Procedure 1: Taking an Order
1. Confirm the product code, size, and colour with the customer.
2. Check stock in the store system. If an item is out of stock, offer an alternative or tell the customer when it is expected.
3. Record the customer's name, phone number, and delivery address.
4. Confirm the total price, the delivery fee, and the payment method.
5. Send an order confirmation by WhatsApp or SMS with the order number.

## Procedure 2: Custom-Stitched Orders
1. Choose the fabric from our collection.
2. Take the customer's measurements (chest, shoulder, sleeve length, qameez length, shalwar length, waist, and neck) at the store, or accept measurements sent by a customer who already has a measurement card on file.
3. Collect the 50% advance (PKR 3,750 for one standard suit).
4. The tailor completes the suit in 5 working days, or in 48 hours if the customer pays the rush fee.
5. The customer pays the remaining balance on pickup or delivery.
Rush orders are not accepted during the last 3 days before Eid.

## Procedure 3: Handling a Return or Exchange
1. Ask for the receipt or order number.
2. Check that the item is unworn, has its tags, and is within the return window.
3. Check whether an exception applies (custom-stitched, watch with opened seal, or clearance item).
4. Approve the return, process the refund or exchange, and note the reason in the return register.
5. Items that look worn or damaged are sent to the manager for a decision.

## Procedure 4: Watch Warranty Claims
1. Ask the customer to bring the watch with the receipt and warranty card to the Lahore main store.
2. The manager inspects the watch for signs of misuse or water damage.
3. Accepted watches are sent to the authorised service partner. Repairs usually take 10 to 14 working days.
4. The customer is called when the watch is ready for collection.
The Rawalpindi branch can accept watches for a warranty claim and forward them to Lahore.

## Procedure 5: Daily Opening and Closing
- The shop opens its doors at the opening time shown in the overview document. The manager counts the cash drawer before opening and after closing.
- Online orders received after 6:00 PM are processed on the next working day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I order on WhatsApp?
Yes. Send your order to 0300-5550142 with the product code, size, colour, and address.

Q: Which shoe size should I choose for Chelsea boots?
If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one.

Q: Do you sell shalwar qameez in children's sizes?
No. Niaz Men's Wear sells men's sizes only.

Q: How do I care for suede boots?
Keep them dry, brush them with a suede brush, and use a protector spray. Do not wash them.

Q: Can I buy a watch and a pair of shoes together and get free delivery?
Free delivery depends on the order total. The total must meet the minimum stated in the current policy.

Q: Is the Orion Automatic watch suitable for swimming?
No. It has a water resistance of 50 metres, which protects against splashes and short immersion, but we do not recommend swimming or showering with it.

Q: Can I pick up my online order from the store?
Yes. Orders can be picked up from the Lahore main store during opening hours, and no delivery fee is charged.

Q: Do I need an appointment for measurements?
No appointment is needed. Walk-ins are welcome at the Lahore main store during opening hours.

Q: Who is the owner?
The store is run by Bilal Niaz. The founder is Haji Niaz Ahmed.
