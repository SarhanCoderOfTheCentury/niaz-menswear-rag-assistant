# Niaz Men's Wear - Company Overview

## About Us
Niaz Men's Wear is a family-owned menswear retailer based in Lahore, Pakistan. The shop was founded in 2011 by Haji Niaz Ahmed and is now run by his son, Bilal Niaz. We sell five product lines: shoes, jackets, sandals, shalwar qameez, and watches. Our aim is to offer good-quality, honestly priced clothing and accessories for everyday wear, office wear, and weddings.

The store has 14 staff members, including four tailors who work on custom-stitched shalwar qameez orders.

## Locations
- Main Store: Shop 14, Liberty Market, Gulberg III, Lahore. This is our flagship store, and the tailoring workshop is located here.
- Branch Store: Shop 7, Bahria Town Commercial Area, Phase 4, Rawalpindi. This branch sells ready-made items only. Custom stitching orders can be placed at the Rawalpindi branch, but they are sent to the Lahore workshop.
- Online: Orders are accepted by phone, WhatsApp, and our website, www.niazmenswear.example.
