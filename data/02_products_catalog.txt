## Size Guide for Shoes
Our shoe sizes follow European numbering. A size 42 corresponds to approximately a foot length of 26.5 cm. If you are between two sizes, we suggest choosing the larger size for Chelsea boots and the smaller size for the Peshawari Chappal, since leather stretches.
