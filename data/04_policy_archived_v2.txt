# Store Policy - Version 2.1 (ARCHIVED)

Version: 2.1 | Effective date: 1 January 2024 | Valid until: 31 August 2026 | Status: ARCHIVED

This policy is no longer in force. It was replaced by Version 3.0 on 1 September 2026. It is kept for reference only and should be used only when someone asks specifically about the old rules, or about a purchase made before 1 September 2026.

## 1. Returns and Exchanges
1.1 Customers could return or exchange an unused item within 3 days of the purchase or delivery date.
1.2 The item had to be unworn, with tags attached and the receipt.
1.3 Approved returns were given as store credit only. Cash or card refunds were not offered.
1.4 Exchanges for a different size were free, subject to stock.

## 2. Exceptions
2.1 Custom-stitched shalwar qameez could not be returned or exchanged. Defects had to be reported within 48 hours.
2.2 Watches could be exchanged only if the box seal was unbroken.
2.3 Clearance items were final sale: no return, no refund, and no exchange.

## 3. Delivery
3.1 Delivery fee: PKR 200 per order.
3.2 Delivery was free for orders of PKR 3,000 or more.
3.3 Delivery time: 2 working days in Lahore and Rawalpindi; 3 to 5 working days to other cities.

## 4. Payment
4.1 Accepted methods: cash, debit and credit cards, and bank transfer. JazzCash and Easypaisa were not accepted.
4.2 Cash on delivery was available for orders of any amount.
4.3 Custom-stitched orders required a 50% advance payment.

## 5. Warranty
5.1 Watches had a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects.
5.2 Shoes and sandals had a 30-day warranty on sole and stitching defects.
5.3 Water damage, accidental damage, and normal wear were not covered.
