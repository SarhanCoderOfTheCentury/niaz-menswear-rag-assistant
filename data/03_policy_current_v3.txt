# Store Policy - Version 3.0 (CURRENT)

Version: 3.0 | Effective date: 1 September 2026 | Status: CURRENT

This is the policy in force today. It replaced Version 2.1 on 1 September 2026. For any question about current rules, use this document.

## 1. Returns and Exchanges
1.1 Customers may return or exchange an unused item within 7 days of the purchase or delivery date.
1.2 The item must be unworn, in its original condition, with tags attached and the receipt or order number.
1.3 Approved returns are refunded to the original payment method within 5 working days. A customer may choose store credit instead, valid for 6 months.
1.4 Exchanges for a different size or colour are free, subject to stock.

## 2. Exceptions to the Return Rules
2.1 Custom-stitched shalwar qameez cannot be returned or exchanged because they are made to individual measurements. The only exception is a stitching or fabric defect, which must be reported within 48 hours of receiving the order. The suit will then be corrected or re-made at no cost.
2.2 Watches can be returned within 7 days only if the box seal is unbroken and the watch has not been worn. Once the seal is opened, a watch can only be handled through the warranty (Section 5).
2.3 Clearance items (marked "Clearance" on the tag) can be exchanged within 7 days but cannot be refunded.

## 3. Delivery
3.1 Delivery fee: PKR 250 per order.
3.2 Delivery is free for orders of PKR 5,000 or more (the total after any discounts).
3.3 Delivery time: 2 working days in Lahore and Rawalpindi; 3 to 5 working days to other cities in Pakistan.
3.4 Custom-stitched items are delivered after the stitching time ends.

## 4. Payment
4.1 Accepted methods: cash, debit and credit cards, JazzCash, Easypaisa, and bank transfer.
4.2 Cash on delivery (COD) is available for orders up to PKR 25,000. Orders above this amount must be paid in advance.
4.3 Custom-stitched orders require a 50% advance payment.

## 5. Warranty
5.1 Watches have a 12-month warranty from the purchase date against manufacturing defects.
5.2 Shoes and sandals have a 30-day warranty on sole and stitching defects.
5.3 The warranty does not cover water damage beyond the stated water resistance, accidental damage, or normal wear.
